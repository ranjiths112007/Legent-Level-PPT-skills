# Prompt Engineering for Presentation Images

Template:
```text
Purpose: [what the visual communicates]
Subject: [specific subject]
Context: [environment/details]
Composition: [placement, negative space, focal point]
Viewpoint: [camera angle/perspective]
Style: [photographic/editorial/isometric/vector/3D]
Lighting: [direction and quality]
Palette: [design-system colors]
Aspect ratio: [target region]
Slide placement: [left/right/full bleed/background]
Text constraints: no embedded text/logos unless required
Avoid: [unwanted objects, clutter, distortion]
```

Reserve negative space where slide text sits. Keep subject away from crop boundaries. Match visual direction to slide flow. Avoid vague prompts, asking image models for accurate charts, long embedded text, incompatible styles, unauthorized logos, and failing to describe text space.

Record prompt, model/version, settings, output, reviewer score, artifacts and use decision.
