# Image Generation Workflow

1. Identify the slide's communication goal.
2. Define visual role: hero, supporting photo, background, illustration or concept.
3. Choose generation, retrieval or native objects.
4. Prompt subject, context, composition, style, lighting, palette, crop and exclusions.
5. Select aspect ratio/resolution for target region.
6. Generate candidate assets.
7. Inspect correctness, artifacts, unwanted text, style consistency and composition.
8. Edit or regenerate.
9. Crop/position without stretching.
10. Optimize and record provenance.
11. Add meaningful alt text.
12. Render the slide and inspect in context.

Provider APIs may expose size, quality, format, compression, transparency/background and editing controls. These vary by model/version; consult current docs.

Create a deck-level consistency brief: style, palette, realism, lighting, camera angle, background, visual motifs and prohibited inconsistencies. Reference images help but do not guarantee consistent outputs.

Risks: wrong subject, text artifacts, distorted details, inconsistent style, subject beneath text, bad crop, low resolution, misleading diagrams, licensing/privacy issues. Realistic appearance is not proof of factual accuracy.
