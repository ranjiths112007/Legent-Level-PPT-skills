# Asset Licensing, Privacy, and Provenance

**Status:** Policy reference  
**Last reviewed:** 2026-11-21

Record creator/provider, source URL, license, retrieval date and attribution. For generated assets, record model/provider, prompt, date and applicable terms.

Do not assume:
- Publicly accessible means free to reuse.
- AI-generated automatically means no rights issues.
- Logos can be copied or regenerated without permission.
- Stock licenses permit redistribution in a public repository.
- Confidential source content may be sent to any image API.

Before external generation, identify sensitive source information, get authorization, minimize prompt data, avoid unnecessary customer/company metrics, and keep secrets out of slides, notes and logs.

Commit only original, appropriately licensed, or explicitly permitted assets. Use synthetic examples.
