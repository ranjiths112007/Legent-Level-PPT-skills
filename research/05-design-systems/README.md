## Tokens

Canvas; safe margins; grid; spacing scale; primary/secondary/accent/background/surface/text/muted/border/status colors; heading/body/label/caption/numeric styles; shape/line styles; chart palette; image treatments; footer and slide-number rules.

## Avoiding template monotony

Maintain patterns for title, section divider, single insight, comparison, process, timeline, metric, chart, table, quote, case study, image-led, and closing slides. Choose by message; do not cycle mechanically.

## Review
Can the audience identify the message quickly? Is the hierarchy obvious? Is the slide overcrowded? Are alignment and gaps consistent? Is color encoding meaningful? Does the layout serve the content? Does the slide advance the narrative?
