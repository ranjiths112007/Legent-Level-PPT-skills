# Layout and Composition

**Status:** Reference  
**Last reviewed:** 2026-11-21

## Layout patterns

Title/thesis; section divider; single insight; comparison; timeline; process/flow; funnel; hierarchy/org chart; KPI; chart with takeaway; table; quote; case study; image-led narrative; map; appendix/references.

## Selection

Choose by semantic intent: sequential, comparative, hierarchical, geographic, quantitative, or illustrative. Ask what audience should notice first, how many content units matter, what must remain editable, and whether a chart/diagram/image is necessary.

## Common defects

Uneven margins, misaligned edges, inconsistent gaps, accidental overlap, content too close to edges, stretched imagery, dense blocks, repeated card grids, competing focal points.

## Algorithm research

Template selection; constraint-based geometry; grid snapping; rectangle packing/collision detection; text measurement and iterative fitting; rule-based placement; LLM-proposed layouts with deterministic validation. Do not rely on LLM intuition for exact geometry.
