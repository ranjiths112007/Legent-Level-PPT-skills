# Visual Hierarchy, White Space, and Spacing

Hierarchy uses size/weight, position/alignment, contrast/color, grouping/proximity, repetition, directional cues and isolation/white space.

White space is functional separation, not wasted space. It reduces competition and clarifies groups.

Use a spacing scale rather than arbitrary values. Keep slide margins, title-to-content gaps, object gaps and chart-label spacing consistent.

There is no universal word-count limit; density depends on audience, display and whether the deck is read or presented.

Review: one primary focal point; meaningful grouping; consistent alignment; safe margins; readable type; no irrelevant decoration; title communicates subject or conclusion.
